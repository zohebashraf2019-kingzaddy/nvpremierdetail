    Gloss

    Arrival

    A typical Reno car: desert dust, road film and water spots dulling the paint. We set up in your driveway with our own purified water and power.

    Foam bath

    Thick pH-neutral foam dwells on every panel and lifts grit before a mitt ever touches the paint. That’s how you avoid wash scratches.

    Decontamination

    Iron remover turns purple as it dissolves embedded brake dust, then a clay bar pulls out what’s left. The paint goes from gritty to glass-smooth.

    Paint correction

    Machine polishing levels the clear coat to remove swirls, water spot etching and Nevada pinstriping, from 1-step to 3-step depending on the paint.

    9H ceramic coating

    The coating is applied panel by panel and cures into a hard, glossy layer that shields against UV, road salt, sap and bird droppings for 1 to 6 years.

    Water beading

    The hydrophobic surface makes water bead up and roll off, carrying dirt with it. Washing takes half the time.

    Wheels & tires

    Barrels and faces deep cleaned, calipers degreased, tires dressed. Wheels can be coated too, so brake dust wipes right off.

    Interior

    Steam, shampoo and leather conditioning for seats, carpets, headliner and every crevice. Interior surfaces can be coated as well.

    Delivered

    Inspected panel by panel before we hand back the keys. This is the finish you can expect.
