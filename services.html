Before you book

A few things that keep the schedule fair for everyone.

  • Booking fee. A non-refundable booking fee confirms your appointment. It goes toward your final price.
  • Arrival window. We keep a 1 to 2 hour window between appointments for delays. Please plan your day around it.
  • Rescheduling. Reschedule at least 48 hours before your appointment.
  • No-shows. We can’t take new bookings from clients who don’t show or call.
