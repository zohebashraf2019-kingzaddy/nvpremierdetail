How a coating goes on

A coating only performs if the paint underneath is right. Every install follows the same four steps.

  1. Decontaminate

    Foam bath, iron remover and clay bar pull out what washing leaves behind.

  2. Correct & polish

    Machine polishing removes swirls and spots so they aren’t sealed in.

  3. Apply & cure

    The coating is applied panel by panel, then left to bond and harden.

  4. Inspect

    We check every panel under light before handing back the keys.
